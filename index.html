About the Parlour

A neighbourhood parlour that knows your occasions by heart

Tucked into Durga Colony near Sonipat Stand, Shaleen Makeup Studio is where Rohtak's women come for honest beauty care — a soft glam look for the evening, a full bridal morning, or simply an hour of grooming that is entirely their own.

Every appointment is unhurried and personal. We listen first, suggest what suits you, and keep the experience calm from the moment you walk in.

  • Ladies-only space, comfortable and private
  • Makeup, hair, skin and grooming under one roof
  • Minutes from New Bus Stand Road — easy to reach
  • Makeup masterclasses for those who want to learn
